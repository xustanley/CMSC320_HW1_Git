Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: 
You are able to maintain a version history over time. 
You can develop new features without breaking the working main version.
You can collaborate with others on a shared project.
You can have others review your work before you contribute to the main version.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer:
Merging combines two branches through a new merge commit, whereas rebasing replays
versions and moving commits onto a new base branch.
